# Prompts de los thumbnails

Cómo usarlos: abrí un chat nuevo con el generador de imágenes, pegá el **prompt base** junto con un thumbnail que te
guste como referencia (el de 100 m es el mejor) y pedí la primera prueba. Después, en el mismo chat, pedí cada una
con su línea de abajo ("Ahora: ..."). Así todas salen del mismo estilo.

Formato: 16:9 horizontal (el juego la recorta a tarjeta chica, así que el atleta va **entero y en el centro**, con
aire alrededor).

## Prompt base

```
Create a 16:9 horizontal illustration in a bright, flat cartoon style with bold black outlines and clean cel shading,
exactly matching the style of the reference image.

The athlete is a stickman-like figure: a perfectly round, glossy, solid black head with NO face (no eyes, no mouth),
and smooth solid black arms and legs with simple muscle shapes. He wears a red sleeveless athletics top with a small
blank white bib patch on the chest (no number, no text), black shorts and white-and-gold running shoes.
Only ONE athlete, shown full body, large, in the center of the image, in a dynamic action pose with a little motion
dust or speed lines. Leave some space around him (the image will be cropped).

Setting: a big outdoor athletics stadium on a sunny day: blue sky with soft white clouds, a red running track with
white lane lines, green infield, packed stands full of colorful cheering cartoon fans, floodlight towers and small
colored flags on the roof. Saturated, cheerful colors, soft daylight from the left.

No text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no brand names, no sponsors anywhere.
Keep the exact same character design, colors, line weight and stadium style in every image of this series.
```

## Una línea por prueba

Pista y campo (estadio de día, como el base):

- **100 m:** `Now: 100 metres sprint. The athlete explodes out of the starting blocks, leaning forward, one knee high, arms pumping; lane lines and a starting block behind him.`
- **110 m con vallas:** `Now: 110 metres hurdles. The athlete is in mid-air clearing a white hurdle, leading leg straight forward, trailing leg bent sideways; more hurdles in a row behind him.`
- **Salto en largo:** `Now: long jump. The athlete flies through the air over the sand pit, arms forward, legs extended, just before landing; the take-off board and sand spray visible.`
- **Salto triple:** `Now: triple jump. The athlete is mid-bound on the runway (the "step" phase), one leg driving forward and the other behind, arms swinging; the sand pit ahead.`
- **Salto en alto:** `Now: high jump, Fosbury flop. The athlete arches backwards over the bar, back to the bar, between two uprights, with the big blue landing mat below.`
- **Garrocha:** `Now: pole vault. The athlete hangs from a long bent pole, swinging up, feet rising toward the bar between tall uprights; the landing mat below.`
- **Jabalina:** `Now: javelin throw. The athlete at the end of his run-up, throwing arm pulled far back holding the javelin, other arm pointing forward, body leaning back.`
- **Martillo:** `Now: hammer throw. The athlete spins inside the throwing circle in front of the safety cage, arms extended holding the hammer cable, the ball swinging around him with a motion blur arc.`
- **Bala:** `Now: shot put. The athlete in the throwing circle pushes the shot from his neck, arm extending, the black ball just leaving his hand, body twisting.`
- **Posta 4x100:** `Now: 4x100 relay. Two identical athletes on the track: one hands a red baton to the other, who is already running forward with his arm back to receive it. (This one has two athletes.)`

Natación (estadio de natación techado, en lugar del estadio de pista):

```
For swimming, change the setting: an indoor Olympic swimming arena, warm lights, packed stands with colorful cheering
fans, flags of many countries hanging from the roof, a turquoise pool with lane ropes (red and white floats), a
numbered starting block at the left edge. The athlete keeps the same black stickman body but wears a red swim cap,
silver goggles and black swim trunks (no top). Water splashes in the same cartoon style.
```

- **Crawl:** `Now: freestyle (crawl). The swimmer reaches far forward with one arm, the other arm recovering over the water, face turned to the side to breathe.`
- **Pecho:** `Now: breaststroke. The swimmer rises out of the water with both arms sweeping forward together, head up, legs in a frog kick under the water.`
- **Mariposa:** `Now: butterfly. The swimmer lunges out of the water with both arms swinging forward over the surface at the same time, chest up, a big splash around.`

Otras:

- **Ciclismo:** `For cycling, change the setting: an indoor wooden velodrome with a steep banked track, blue and red lines on the wood, packed stands and bright roof lights. Now: track cycling. The athlete rides a track bike low and fast on the banking, wearing a red-and-black aerodynamic helmet and a black skinsuit with red details, speed lines behind.`
- **Laser Run:** `Now: laser run (modern pentathlon). The athlete stands at the shooting line wearing a red cap, aiming a laser pistol with one straight arm at a white target board with five round black targets; a thin red laser line goes to one target. Other small athletes run on the track in the background.`

Pantallas del menú:

- **Torneo:** `Now: a tournament poster: the athlete stands on top of the podium raising a big golden trophy with both arms, confetti falling, the stadium cheering behind him.`
- **Supervivencia (menú):** `Now: survival mode: the athlete runs straight toward the viewer, determined, with a few glowing red "machine" rival silhouettes chasing close behind him.`
- **Supervivencia clásica:** `Same scene as survival, but as a vintage 1980s photo-poster: warm faded colors, film grain, slightly blurry, retro stadium.`
- **Supervivencia moderna:** `Same scene as survival, but futuristic: night stadium with neon blue and red lights, glossy track, the rivals as bright digital silhouettes.`

## Si una sale distinta

Pedile en el mismo chat: `Redo it keeping exactly the same character (round black faceless head, red top with a blank white patch, black shorts, white-gold shoes), the same outline thickness and the same stadium as the first image.`
